QuietType Privacy Policy

Effective October 6, 2026

In short: what you write in QuietType stays on your computer. QuietType has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it never connects to the internet. The only time your words leave your computer is when you choose to search for them, and then your web browser does the searching.

What QuietType keeps, and where

Everything below is stored only on your own computer, in places you control.

  • Your current draft, saved as you type so nothing is lost if QuietType closes. It lives in QuietType's app data folder.
  • Your settings, such as theme, text size and shortcut, in the same folder.
  • Recovery copies, dated copies of your writing saved every couple of minutes and before the page is cleared, in Documents\QuietType\Recovery. The newest 200 are kept.
  • A copy of what you send. Each time you press Ctrl + Enter, the text is added to a dated file in Documents\QuietType. You can turn this off from the tray icon (“Keep a copy of what I send”).
  • Word files you save with Ctrl + S, in the folder you choose.
  • A troubleshooting log in the app data folder. It records events such as “copied” or “pasted” and how many characters were involved, never the words themselves.

You can delete any of these at any time. Uninstalling the Microsoft Store version removes the app data folder. Files in your Documents folder stay until you delete them.

The clipboard

QuietType uses the Windows clipboard to move words in and out:

  • When you open QuietType with its keyboard shortcut and you have text highlighted in another app, QuietType presses Ctrl + C for you to bring that text in, then puts back whatever was on your clipboard before. You can turn this off from the tray icon (“Bring in highlighted text when I open it”).
  • When you press Ctrl + Enter, QuietType puts your writing on the clipboard, returns to the window you were using and presses Ctrl + V for you. You can turn off the automatic paste from the tray icon (“Paste for me when I finish”).

To return you to the right place, QuietType notes which window was in front when you opened it. That's kept in memory only for that moment and is never saved.

Web search

If you highlight words and press Ctrl + K, QuietType opens your web browser with a Google search for those words. QuietType sends nothing itself. Your browser does the search, and Google's privacy policy applies to it. Nothing is searched unless you press Ctrl + K.

What QuietType does not do

  • It makes no network connections and sends nothing to its maker or anyone else.
  • It has no accounts, sign-in, advertising, analytics or crash reporting of its own.
  • It doesn't sell, share or look at your writing.
  • It doesn't use AI, spellcheck services or writing assistants. By default it also keeps tools like Grammarly out of its page.

The Microsoft Store

If you got QuietType from the Microsoft Store, Microsoft handles purchases, downloads and updates, and may collect information about them under the Microsoft Privacy Statement. The maker of QuietType sees only the reports Microsoft gives every app publisher, such as how many people installed QuietType and whether it crashed.

Children

QuietType collects no personal information from anyone, including children.
