## What A Website From Me Looks Like

**Live in two weeks.** Fixed scope, fixed price, a date you can put in a calendar. No open-ended project that quietly runs six months.

**You can edit it yourself.** Hours, staff, service times, the menu, a new event — you change those without phoning me. That's the whole design goal.

**It works on a phone.** Most of the people looking for you are standing in a parking lot holding a phone. That's the version I build first.

**It doesn't rot.** Hosting, security updates, backups and your domain are handled. You don't get a call in three years telling you the site is down and nobody knows who has the password.

## What I've Built

**[Father Trucker](https://thefathertrucker.ca)** — A ministry site for long-haul truckers, built for someone reading on a phone in a cab at a truck stop. Fast, easy to read, and the ministry team updates it themselves without needing me. That last part is the part I'm proudest of.

**[Conversational AI Agents](https://agents.davidwicks.site)** — Voice agents that answer the questions your office keeps answering: service times, room bookings, who to call about a funeral. They run as an embedded widget or through a WordPress plugin I wrote, so your staff can manage them without phoning me. There's a live demo you can talk to.

**[Three-Year Bible Reading Plan](https://readingplan.kzmc.ca)** — Built for my own congregation and still running every week. Each week pairs a New Testament reading with an Old Testament chapter on the same theme, so people who can't sustain a heavy plan still get the through-line instead of stalling out partway through Leviticus.

There's more — sports software, habit tracking, a digital detox program — over on [projects](/projects/).
