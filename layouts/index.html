Why hire me over an agency.

The honest version
Domain knowledge

I already speak your language

Twelve years leading congregations. I know what a board meeting sounds like, why volunteer turnover breaks your process, and which "simple" request is going to cost you three months. You won't spend the first two weeks explaining your world to me.

Scope

You get the person who writes the code

No account manager, no handoff, no junior developer learning on your budget. The person you talk to on Tuesday is the person shipping on Thursday.

Judgment

I'll tell you not to build it

Plenty of problems get solved by a spreadsheet, a free tool, or changing a process. I'd rather lose a project than take money for software you didn't need — and I've turned down work on exactly those grounds.

Range

The unusual stuff is where I'm strongest

Molecular biology, counselling, pastoral work, welding, software. That range isn't a party trick — it means when a project has a physical component, a human component and a technical one, I'm not handing off two thirds of it.
